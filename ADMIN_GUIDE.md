# Slavic Sliders: website editing guide

This guide is for updating the website. You don't need any technical knowledge.
Everything works from a computer **or your phone**.

---

## Logging in

1. Open the admin link you were sent (it ends in **/admin**), for example `http://192.168.1.50:3000/admin`
2. Enter your email and password
3. You'll see the **dashboard**: a list of everything you can edit, grouped into sections:

| Section | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Team** | Drivers, Cars, Parts, Build log |
| **Photos & Videos** | Photos, Videos |
| **Racing** | Tracks, Events |
| **Sponsors** | Sponsors |
| **Inbox** | Messages from the website forms, and the link-click report |

On a phone, tap the **☰ menu** at the top left to see these sections.

**The golden rule:** after changing anything, press the blue **Save** button (top right).
Changes show up on the website right away.

Every page has a **Preview** button (top right, next to Save) that opens that item on the
real website so you can check how it looks.

Every box has a short hint underneath it explaining what to type. If in doubt, read the hint.

## Uploading photos

You can add photos in two ways:

- **Straight from a form** (for example the "Driver photo" box on a driver). Tap
  **Create new**, then drag the photo in, or tap to choose it from your phone's camera roll.
- **In bulk:** go to **Photos & Videos → Photos → Create new**, then drag in a photo.

For each photo:

1. **Describe the photo** (required): one sentence describing what's in it, e.g.
   *"Blue 350Z drifting at the hairpin with tire smoke."* This helps blind visitors and Google.
2. **Caption** (optional): shows under the photo in the gallery.
3. **Show in the Gallery**: leave this **on** for action shots. Turn it **off** for logos,
   headshots or graphics.
4. **Tags**: pick the **Track**, **Driver(s)** and **Event** so people can filter the gallery.
5. **Save**

Don't worry about photo size. Big phone photos are shrunk and compressed automatically.

> Tip: after uploading, you can tap on the photo preview and drag the little dot (the **focal point**)
> onto the most important part (the car). That part stays visible when the photo gets cropped.

## Add a driver

1. **Team → Drivers → Create new**
2. Fill in:
   - **Driver name** (required)
   - **Nickname** (optional)
   - **Car number**: just the number, e.g. `77`
   - **Years drifting**: just the number, e.g. `6`
   - **Driver photo**: tap and upload (see *Uploading photos*)
   - **Short bio**: a few sentences
   - **Social media links**: open this section and paste their full Instagram / TikTok / YouTube links
   - **Display order** (right side): `1` shows first on the website, `2` second, and so on
3. **Save**

The driver now has their own page on the website. Next, add their car.

## Add a car

1. **Team → Cars → Create new**
2. **Driver**: pick the driver from the dropdown
3. **Status** (right side): **Current car**, or **Past car** if it's sold or retired
4. **Year / Make / Model / Generation**: e.g. `2002` / `Ford` / `Mustang` / `SN95 New Edge`
5. **Main photo**: the big photo at the top of the car's page. Sideways (landscape) photos look best.
6. **More photos**: add as many as you like. Drag them to change the order.
7. **About this car**: a short description
8. **Specs**: Engine, Power, Transmission (e.g. `4.6L 2V turbo`, `420 whp`, `T56 6-speed`)
9. **Years owned** (right side): e.g. `2021–present`
10. Set the paint (next section), then **Save**

### Set the car's paint

The paint shows as a small color dot next to the car's name all over the site.

In the **Paint / wrap** section:

1. **Paint name**: e.g. `Navy` or `Mystichrome wrap`
2. **Paint type**:
   - **Solid**: one color
   - **Color-shift** or **Wrap**: 2 to 4 colors (shown as a blend)
3. Under **Colors**, tap **Add Color**. Tap the colored square to pick a color, or paste a
   color code like `#1B2A5C`.
   - Solid: add **exactly 1** color
   - Color-shift / Wrap: add **2 to 4** colors, in order

If you add the wrong number of colors, a red message will tell you when you save.

## Mark a car as "Past" (sold or retired)

1. **Team → Cars**, then tap the car
2. On the right, change **Status** to **Past car (sold / retired)**
3. Update **Years owned**, e.g. `2017–2020`
4. **Save**

The car moves to the driver's **Past cars** tab, where it shows in black & white until someone
hovers over it.

## Add a part (with an Amazon link)

1. **Team → Parts → Create new**
2. **Car**: pick which car it's on
3. **Part name**: e.g. `Coilovers`
4. **Brand**: e.g. `BC Racing`
5. **Category**: pick one (Suspension, Engine, Brakes…)
6. **Notes** (optional): e.g. `12k front / 8k rear springs`
7. Open **Store link (optional)**:
   - **Store link**: paste the Amazon link here. Use the **Get link** button in the
     Amazon Associates bar at the top of the Amazon page (called *SiteStripe*), so the link
     includes our Associates tag and we get credit.
   - **Store**: leave on **Amazon**
8. **Save**

Parts with a link get a **Buy on Amazon** button. Parts without a link just show the name.

**Please don't:**
- upload pictures you copied from Amazon (not allowed by Amazon's rules). Use your own photos or none.
- remove the "As an Amazon Associate…" message. The site adds it automatically and it's required.

## Add a build log post

Build log posts are updates about a car ("New angle kit installed", "Engine swap done").
They show as a timeline on the car's page.

1. **Team → Build log → Create new**
2. Pick the **Car**, write a **Title**, set the **Date**
3. Write what happened in **What happened?** (you can use bold, lists, links…)
4. Add **Photos** if you have them
5. **Save**

---

## Add a YouTube or Instagram video

Videos aren't uploaded to the website. Post them on YouTube or Instagram first, then link them.

1. On YouTube or Instagram, open the video and tap **Share → Copy link**
2. In the admin: **Photos & Videos → Videos → Create new**
3. **Video title**: e.g. `Tandem runs at Evergreen`
4. **YouTube or Instagram link**: paste the link
5. **Date filmed**
6. **Tags**: pick the track, drivers and event
7. **Save**

It now appears in the **Gallery** (and on the home page if it's one of the newest).

## Add a sponsor

1. **Sponsors → Sponsors → Create new**
2. **Company name**
3. **Logo**: upload their logo. A PNG with a see-through background is best. White logos are
   fine (they sit on a dark background). On the logo, turn **Show in the Gallery** off.
4. **Website link**: e.g. `https://fifthwheelllc.com`
5. **Sponsor level**: **Title** (biggest, featured), **Gold** or **Supporting**
6. **One-line description**: e.g. `Car transportation`
7. **Display order**: lower numbers show first
8. **Save**

### Showing sponsors their traffic

Every click on a sponsor's logo is counted. Go to **Inbox → Link Clicks**. The table at the top
shows **clicks per link over the last 30 days**. Screenshot it and send it to the sponsor.

## Add an event

1. **Racing → Events → Create new**
2. **Event name**: e.g. `Pro-Am Round 2`
3. **Date**
4. **Status**: **Upcoming**
5. **Track**: pick from the dropdown (add the track first under **Racing → Tracks** if it's new)
6. **Details**: times, tickets, which drivers are entering
7. **Save**

The next upcoming event shows automatically on the home page.

**After the event:** open it again, change **Status** to **Past (done)**, write how it went
under **Results**, and **Save**. Then tag that day's photos and videos with the event.

---

## Add a track

1. **Racing → Tracks → Create new**
2. **Track name**, **City, State**, a **Track photo**, and a short description
3. **Save**

To show photos and videos on a track's section, tag them with that track when you upload them.

---

## Messages from the website (Inbox)

**Inbox → Inquiries** has every message from the **Contact** and **Become a Sponsor** forms.
Open one to read it. Reply by email (click their address), then set **Status** to **Replied**
and **Save** so the team knows it's handled.

---

## Things only the Admin can change

Ask the team Admin if you need any of these:

- The team **logo**, **tagline**, home-page **background photo/video**
- Team **social links** and **contact email**
- The **Become a Sponsor** page text, stats and packages
- Turning on the **Merch** page
- Adding or removing **logins**

---

## Help, I made a mistake!

- **Haven't saved yet?** Just leave the page. Nothing changes until you press Save.
- **Saved something wrong?** Open it, fix it, and Save again.
- **Deleted something by accident?** Tell the Admin. There are nightly backups.
- **Upload failing?** The photo might be in an unusual format. Take a screenshot of it, or
  export it as a JPG, and try again.
- **Forgot your password?** Ask the Admin to set a new one for you.
